# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short adaptation; the full text at that link applies for anything not covered here.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and constructive; assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; focus on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' contributions.
- Ask when unsure; help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer at **anshul.tripathi.dev@gmail.com**. Reports are handled promptly, fairly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
